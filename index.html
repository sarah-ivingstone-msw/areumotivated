The Connection Hour

Help your people handle stress before it becomes burnout.

The Connection Hour uses guided, discussion-based learning to help teams strengthen practical skills around communication, boundaries, conflict, coping, resilience, and the pressures of work and life.

Bring The Connection Hour to Your Organization

For organizations • workplaces • nonprofits • small groups  |  In-person & virtual

How It Works

One hour. Real conversation. Practical skills.

Participants do not just listen to another presentation. Sarah facilitates guided discussion around real-life challenges and practical skills participants can use beyond the session.

Topics include:

Stress & Burnout Communication Boundaries Conflict Resilience Healthy Habits Emotional Awareness Managing Change
