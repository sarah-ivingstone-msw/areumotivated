The Connection Hour

Practical skills for handling life before stress becomes burnout.

Life asks a lot of us — but we are not always taught the practical skills that help us manage it. The Connection Hour creates space to learn and practice practical skills for navigating real-life demands.

For young people, it is a chance to build those skills early. For adults, it is a chance to strengthen them when life is already demanding more.

How It Works

One hour. Real conversation. Practical skills.

A 3-Session Pilot Experience

Give your group an opportunity to experience The Connection Hour through three facilitated sessions focused on practical skills they can use in everyday life.

Across three sessions, participants have time to talk, reflect, practice, and connect what they are learning to real situations. The pilot also gives your organization an opportunity to experience the approach before deciding whether a longer series is right for your group.
