The Connection Hour for Workplaces

Adults are already navigating work, relationships, responsibilities, and competing demands. The Connection Hour gives them an opportunity to strengthen practical skills they can use while navigating those demands.

Why It Matters

When the Pressure Doesn't Let Up

Life places real demands on people — and work adds its own layer of pressure on top of everything else. When people are not equipped with practical skills for managing stress, communicating effectively, and maintaining healthy boundaries, those demands can compound over time and contribute to burnout.

The Connection Hour is not another passive presentation your team sits through and forgets. Sarah facilitates real conversation around the pressures your people actually face — and walks participants through practical skills they can apply in their work and lives immediately.

This is practical, discussion-based education. Not therapy. Not a seminar. A real conversation with real tools.

Not another presentation. Guided discussion and practical skills your team can actually use.

  • Discussion-based, not lecture-based
  • Facilitated by Sarah Livingstone, MSW
  • In-person or virtual delivery
  • Adaptable for your industry and team
  • Not therapy — practical skill-building

What Participants Practice

Skills That Transfer to Real Life

The Connection Hour draws from a curriculum of 13 discussion topics, adapted to fit the needs of your team or organization.

Dealing with Stress

Recognizing stress as it builds and developing practical tools for managing it before it becomes overwhelming or leads to burnout.

Reaction vs. Response

Practicing the pause between feeling something and acting on it — a foundational skill for healthier workplace communication.

Conflict Resolution

Moving from reactive responses to thoughtful ones — practical tools for navigating disagreement at work without damaging relationships.

Boundaries / Survival / Growth

Recognizing and communicating personal and professional limits — and understanding why healthy boundaries support long-term functioning.

Resilience & Protection

Building the capacity to recover from setbacks, adapt to change, and maintain functioning when work life gets demanding.

The Power of Habits

Identifying the everyday habits that support sustained energy, focus, and emotional well-being — and making small, practical changes.

Concern & Empathy

Building the capacity to understand others' experiences and communicate that understanding — an essential skill in collaborative environments.

Avoiding Self-Sabotage

Recognizing the patterns that get in the way of professional growth and relationships — and building healthier alternatives.

Managing Change

Navigating workplace transitions, uncertainty, and the emotional weight of change with greater skill and steadiness.
